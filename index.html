From the hematology bench to the command line.

I started out in a hematology lab, running blood tests for patients with leukemia. Today I write the software that reads their genomes.

I trained in Medical Laboratory Sciences and Human Genetics, and my work now sits where clinical genetics meets software engineering. I design scalable Nextflow pipelines, benchmark variant callers against GIAB truth sets, and write clinical reports that help families get a diagnosis.

My research covers hereditary spastic paraplegia, rare neurological disorders, cancer transcriptomics, and endogenous retroviruses in Parkinson’s disease at single-cell resolution.
